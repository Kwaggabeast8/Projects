# AI visuals for Madhaus Nutrition

Copy-paste prompts for Higgsfield, or any free generator (Ideogram, Leonardo, Bing Image Creator,
Kling, Luma, Hailuo...). Free tiers are credit-limited, so check each tool's current limits.

**Brand look (paste at the end of every prompt):**
`dark black studio background, deep red rim lighting, subtle neon lime-yellow (#c8ff00) accent glow, high contrast, cinematic, gritty gym aesthetic, premium sports nutrition brand, no text, no logos`

## The one rule for supplements

AI scrambles label text. **Never let AI draw your real label.** Photograph your actual tub/bag (phone + window light is fine),
cut out the background, and use AI for *backgrounds, lifestyle and video* around it. Do not use AI to imply results,
doctor endorsements or fake customers.

## Tips

- Generate 4-8 variations per prompt and pick the best. Keep the same brand-look line so everything matches.
- Keep the left or centre of hero images empty, because the headline sits there.
- Compress before upload: images to JPG (about 200-400 KB), video to H.264 MP4 (TinyPNG / HandBrake are free).
- Higgsfield / Kling image-to-video works well: feed a still, ask for "slow push-in, particles drifting".
